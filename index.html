The push at home · one example: medicine
Why leave? Often, no seat here.
24 lakh registered for NEET-UG in 2024. Each figure is 10,000 students.
13 lakh qualified.
23,33,297 sat the exam. 13,16,268 cleared the cut-off.
There were about 1.18 lakh MBBS seats.
